# Business Insights

## 1. UX & Website Optimization

The `Checkout Start → Purchase` stage shows strong conversion performance. This means that the checkout flow is likely working well from a technical and UX perspective.

Recommendation: avoid major redesigns of the checkout process at this stage. Focus on monitoring stability and making only small, controlled improvements, since the current flow already converts users effectively.

## 2. Marketing Strategy

Social Media generates a large share of traffic, but its conversion into purchases is relatively weak. This suggests that the channel brings volume, but not always high-intent users.

Recommendation: optimize Social Media acquisition strategy by focusing on traffic quality rather than only traffic volume. Consider retargeting campaigns, lead magnets, email capture, and audience segmentation to convert users later in the funnel.

## 3. Finance & Revenue

The average order value is around `$115`. If customer acquisition cost from Social Media is around `$40`, profitability depends heavily on product margin.

Recommendation: analyze unit economics by traffic source. If gross margin is below approximately 35%, some Social Media transactions may be unprofitable. Track AOV, CAC, ARPU, ARPPU and margin together before scaling ad spend.
